Privacy notice

For the Patched.im free CVE assessment. Last updated: .

This notice covers the personal data processed when you use the free CVE assessment flow at dashboard.patched.im/public/scan. It is written for plain readability rather than legal density. The Data Protection Act 2018 (Isle of Man) applies; the UK GDPR is mirrored.

Who is the controller

{{ legal_name }}, a company registered in the Isle of Man. Contact: hello@patched.im.

What we collect

  • Your email address: used to verify the request and deliver your report.
  • Your company name: used to label the report.
  • The uploaded Intune devices export (CSV): typically contains device names, operating systems, OS versions, compliance state, last sync timestamps, and may contain user principal names, model and manufacturer details, and serial numbers.
  • The optional detected-apps export (JSON): typically contains installed application names and versions per device.
  • Technical metadata: your IP address (used to apply rate limits) and basic request metadata.

We do not require an account. We do not collect any personal data beyond what is listed above.

Lawful basis

Your consent (you upload the data and confirm you are authorised to share it) is the basis for processing the uploaded inventory. Our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of the service is the basis for processing the IP address and rate-limit metadata.

What we do with it

  • Parse the uploaded export to identify device operating systems and installed applications.
  • Cross-reference identified software against the NIST National Vulnerability Database (NVD). The CPE (software identifier) strings are sent to NVD; device names, user names, and other identifiers are not.
  • Generate a branded report and email it to the address you provided, using SendGrid as our email delivery provider. Only your email address is shared with SendGrid for this purpose.
  • Apply rate limits to prevent abuse.

Third parties

  • NIST NVD: receives CPE strings only (e.g. cpe:2.3:a:microsoft:edge:120.0:*:...), no device or user identifiers.
  • Microsoft 365: your email address, the verification code, and the report attachment pass through Patched's own Microsoft 365 mailbox for delivery, hosted in the UK.
  • Cloudflare: fronts the dashboard and, if the Turnstile bot challenge is enabled, sets cookies for bot-detection purposes. See Cloudflare's own privacy notice for details.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any other third party.

Where it is stored

On {{ legal_name }} infrastructure hosted at Hetzner Online GmbH, in the EU. The report is also emailed to you as an attachment through Patched's Microsoft 365 mailbox, hosted in the UK — see Third parties above.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded files (devices CSV, detected-apps JSON): deleted from disk immediately after the report is generated.
  • The generated report: kept for 30 days after generation, then automatically deleted by a daily retention sweep. Sooner if you click the "delete now" link in the report email.
  • Database row (request status, email, company name): marked deleted at the same time as the report. Logical retention beyond that is for abuse-prevention only; personal data is removed.

Your rights

You can:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion (the "delete now" link in your report email does this directly; you can also email us).
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Isle of Man Information Commissioner if you believe we have not handled your data correctly.

Email hello@patched.im to exercise any of these rights. We aim to respond within one calendar month.

Cookies

The CVE assessment flow itself does not set any cookies. If the Cloudflare Turnstile bot-challenge is enabled on the form, Cloudflare may set cookies on the challenge page only.

Changes

If we change this notice we will update the "last updated" date above. Substantive changes that affect existing reports will be notified by email to the address on record.

Contact

{{ legal_name }}, Isle of Man. hello@patched.im.
