What happens to my data?
The uploaded export and generated report are stored on Patched.im infrastructure (Hetzner, EU). The report is also emailed to you as an attachment through Patched's Microsoft 365 mailbox, hosted in the UK. Uploads are deleted immediately after the report is generated. The stored copy is auto-deleted after {{ retention_days }} days, or sooner if you click the "delete now" link in the report email. Patched.im does not share your data with third parties beyond the NVD lookup (which uses CPE strings only, no device or user identifiers). Read the full privacy notice.
