What this page shows

Under Cyber Essentials v3.3, a critical or high severity update outside 14 days of its advisory is an automatic failure, and Cyber Essentials treats an unknown or non-reporting device as not passing. This page states what the evidence Patched holds shows for each device, not a certification verdict. Only a Cyber Essentials assessor certifies an estate, and Patched does not close the 14-day obligation for you. Remediation is routed through your own Intune, Jamf, Patch My PC or Robopack.

Due dates on this page are counted from when each advisory was published, not from when a vendor actually shipped the fix. This can make an item look more overdue than it really is against the true fix release date.
