Software reports

Pre-staged software asset management reports, refreshed automatically from your estate's software and lifecycle evidence.

  • Unsupported software: every installed product and operating system version past its vendor's end of support, or within 90 days of it, with the Cyber Essentials evidence it feeds.
  • Remote access tools: every remote access tool on your estate with its approval state, and your own RMM agents shown separately.
  • Version sprawl: how many versions of each application are live across your estate, and how they compare with the latest released version.
  • Duplicate tools: categories where your estate runs several competing products for the same job, so consolidation candidates are visible without needing price data.
  • Software publishers: every installed application grouped by the publisher its code signing evidence proves, with installs Patched cannot verify shown first.
  • New software: products first seen on your estate since your last review, with quick approve or unapprove from the row.
  • Unapproved and unreviewed software: the current Unapproved and Unreviewed list, a 26-week trend, and the devices carrying the most Unapproved software.
  • Approved software list: every approved application, browser extension and Homebrew policy. Download CSV.

Endpoint assurance

Evidence packs drawn from what Patched already holds about your estate, with the source and date of every answer.

  • Cyber insurance evidence: the endpoint questions on a typical cyber insurance renewal form, each answered from Patched's evidence with how much of your estate it covers, plus the questions Patched cannot answer for you. Download it as a PDF or CSV.
  • Cyber Essentials readiness: what stands between each device and the Cyber Essentials 14-day update rule, and the controls Patched can evidence.
