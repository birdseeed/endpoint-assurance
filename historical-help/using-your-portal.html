Guide 10

Using your Patched portal

The portal at app.patched.im is a read-only view of your own estate: posture, devices, findings and reports, all in one place. This is a short tour of what's on each tab.

Signing in

You sign in with your existing Microsoft or Google account. There's no separate portal password to create or remember. Every session only ever shows your own organisation's data; it's scoped to your tenant on our side, so there's nothing to configure and no risk of seeing (or being seen by) another customer's estate.

Overview

Your landing page. It shows your CIS Level 1 security-posture score as a dial, how that score has moved over time, and a breakdown of open findings by severity: critical, high, medium and low. Alongside it sits inventory freshness, OS currency and the controls requiring attention. For a fuller explanation of what the score and trend mean, see how to read your report.

Devices

The roster of every device in your estate: hostname, platform, OS version, when it was last seen, its own CIS score, and its encryption and OS status. A stale devices view surfaces machines that haven't reported in for a while, so nothing goes quietly unmonitored. You can export the full roster as CSV whenever you need it outside the portal.

Extensions

An inventory of every browser extension installed across your estate, with the browsers and devices each one is on. Policy status is shown alongside discovery evidence so administrators can review what is present and route any required action through browser or endpoint-management policy.

Findings

CVE correlations are shown with the affected software identity, observed version, device count and available confidence evidence. Treat a finding as a prioritised lead when identity or version evidence is incomplete, not as an unqualified proof of exposure.

Software requiring review

Discovered software can be grouped by policy state, including unapproved, prohibited, under review and unclassified. The customer owns those decisions; Patched preserves the discovery and review evidence.

Reports

Generate an audit-ready PDF of your posture on demand, and download reports you've generated previously. You can also schedule recurring delivery (daily, weekly or monthly), so a fresh report lands without you having to ask for it. See how to read your report for a walk-through of what's in it.

Agent enrolment

The enrolment area issues a single-use enrolment token (valid for around 24 hours) along with ready-to-use deploy artifacts for each platform: a macOS script you upload to Intune as a Platform Script, and a Windows msiexec command, each with the token already built in. The agent itself (provided by Patched during onboarding) uses that token the first time it runs to enrol the device and collect its own credentials automatically; the full walk-through of deployment is in Getting started. This enrols the Patched collector. It is not an application store or a route for deploying third-party software.

Support

A direct way to reach us if something needs attention: a question about a finding, a device that isn't reporting, or anything else about your estate.
