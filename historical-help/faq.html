FAQ

The questions buyers and IT teams ask most often, before and during a rollout. Don't see yours? Email hello@patched.im.

Privacy & data

What data does the agent collect?

Device and posture data: hardware identity and specs, OS version, installed applications and their versions, pending and historical updates, certificate expiry, and the CIS security signals (encryption, firewall, screen-lock, and so on). Everything maps to a reporting or compliance purpose. See The endpoint agent for the full list.

Does it read personal files, browsing or keystrokes?

No. The agent does not capture documents, messages, browsing history, keystrokes or screen contents. It is not employee-surveillance tooling, and our acceptable-use terms prohibit using it that way. Controls that could touch user-specific data are flagged and are not switched on without a documented reason and your agreement.

Where is my data stored?

On Patched-operated infrastructure hosted in the EU (Finland and Germany), in a PostgreSQL database, with encrypted nightly backups. Patched is built and run from the Isle of Man by Patched.im Ltd, company number 139269C. We can walk you through the data-flow and retention specifics for a security review.

How is the data protected in transit?

Every upload runs over HTTPS and is independently authenticated (a per-tenant bearer token) and integrity-signed (HMAC-SHA256 over the exact payload), with timestamp and one-time-nonce checks to prevent replay. A request that fails any check is rejected before any data is read.

Platforms & requirements

Which platforms are supported?

macOS and Windows. Both run the same shape of inventory collection and are scored against their respective CIS Level 1 benchmark, so a mixed estate sits on one consistent posture.

Do I need an MDM like Intune or Jamf?

No. Intune or Jamf can deploy the collector if you already use them, but a credible Patched inventory does not require an MDM. Your existing management platform remains the authority for deployment and remediation.

How do I deploy it across the estate?

Through Intune, using the enrolment artifact generated from the portal's Install tab, or by running that same artifact directly on unmanaged machines. The deployment route and permissions are reviewed during onboarding. Both paths are covered in Getting started.

What does the agent need on the network?

All agent traffic is outbound HTTPS (port 443) only. Nothing on the device listens for inbound connections: no inbound firewall rules, no ports to open.

The agent honours system proxy settings (WinHTTP / PAC on Windows; networksetup on macOS), a manually configured proxy, or a direct connection, all via the signed agent configuration.

Do I need to configure my antivirus or EDR?

Do not add blanket exclusions by default. If an AV or EDR product blocks the collector, capture the alert and contact Patched so the exact signed binary, path and required access can be reviewed with the endpoint-security owner.

Compliance & reporting

What exactly does "compliant" mean?

That the device passed the CIS Level 1 controls that apply to it, scored against your policy, with controls we couldn't verify handled conservatively. The full explanation (including how it maps to NIST, ISO 27001 and Cyber Essentials) is in What "Compliant" means.

How often is the data refreshed?

The agent uploads roughly every 15 minutes and re-scans on a schedule, so the dashboard reflects close-to-current posture rather than a monthly snapshot. Scheduled PDF reports go out on whatever cadence you choose.

Can I get reports for an auditor or board?

Yes: a per-device report listing every failing control with the observed value and remediation, and an executive summary that rolls the estate up into KPIs and a prioritised to-do list. Both are designed to be handed straight to an assessor.

What if a device is switched off for a week?

Nothing breaks. The device simply stops uploading while it's off, and its dashboard entry and per-device appendix row show the last check-in date so you can see how stale that device's data is. As soon as it's switched on again and back online, the agent resumes uploading on its normal schedule and its posture reflects the latest snapshot from then on. A laptop offline for annual leave or sitting unused in a drawer just won't contribute a fresh reading until it reconnects.

How do I cancel?

Offboarding and commercial notice follow the terms agreed for the customer. Patched supplies the device and data-removal steps and preserves required audit evidence only for the agreed retention period.

Can I export my data?

Yes. On request we'll provide your full inventory, compliance history, and report archive as CSV/JSON, or as the PDF reports you've already been sent. This is also covered under the DPA's data subject rights: export or deletion within 30 days of your written instruction. See Data & privacy for the detail.
