Once issued, it does not change
The numbers are frozen at the moment of issue, and the coverage figure is stored with them, so a later improvement never rewrites history.
A content hash is printed on the document, so a customer can prove the copy their assessor holds is the copy that was issued.
A correction is a new report that supersedes the old one, never an edit. Both stay in the list.
