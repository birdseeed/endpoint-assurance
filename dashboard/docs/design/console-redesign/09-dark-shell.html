The decision this artboard exists to settle
In the dark token set the rail and the card surface are both #15181e while the canvas is #0f1115, so a straight inversion makes the rail look like a floating card and the page background the darkest thing on screen.
The rule that keeps both themes coherent: the rail is always the darkest surface, and cards are always the lightest. In light that is slate against paper against white. In dark the rail takes the canvas value with a border, and cards lift to #15181e.
Also different in dark
ACCENTSignal lightens to #ff7840 and solid buttons take dark labels, because orange on dark cannot carry white text at this size.
STATUSDanger, warning and success move to their lighter dark-theme values and their washes become low alpha tints rather than pale fills.
ROWSTable headers and hover use #2a2d33, not a lighter card, so a highlighted row never outshines the data in it.
